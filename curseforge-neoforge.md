# Brewing+ (Neoforge)

Never look up a potion recipe again. Click the potion button on the
brewing stand, pick any potion, and see its full recipe, which ingredients
you carry, and what to add next.

- Every potion, splash and lingering form; ingredient lookup and filters
- Track a potion: the stand shows the next ingredient in its slot

Requires **Mycel**. Needs NeoForge 26.3.0.1-beta+, Minecraft 26.3, Java 25.
