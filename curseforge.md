# Brewing+

Never look up a potion recipe again. Click the potion button on the
brewing stand, pick any potion, and see its full recipe from a water
bottle, which ingredients you carry, and what to add next. Track it and
the stand shows the next ingredient right in its slot. Client side, no
keybinds.

- Every potion, splash and lingering form the world can brew
- Full recipe chain with ingredient counts from your inventory
- "Have all" filter: only potions you can brew right now
- Search by potion or ingredient ("carrot" finds Night Vision)
- Ingredient lookup: every recipe an ingredient is used in
- Track a potion: ghost ingredient and next-step card at the stand
- Reads your world's real recipes in singleplayer, datapacks included
- Config: `enabled`, `showButton`, `showStandHint`
- Requires **Mycel**: install it alongside this mod

Reload live with `/mycel reload brewingplus`.
Docs: https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/brewingplus/
